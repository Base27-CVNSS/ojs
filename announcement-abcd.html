Special Issue · Call for Abstracts

Asset-based and community-led development in Ethiopia

19 May 2025College of Social Sciences and HumanitiesAddis Ababa University
ClosedThis is an archived announcement. The abstract deadline was 30 June 2025 and the Q&A session was held on 16 June 2025.

The Ethiopian Journal of the Social Sciences and Humanities invited potential authors to contribute academic papers and reflections on practice to a special issue on Asset-Based and Community-led Development (ABCD) in Ethiopia.

Guest editors were Wassie Kebede of Addis Ababa University and Wellington Sousa of the Coady Institute, St. Francis Xavier University. Contributions from practitioners, community development leaders and academics were welcomed, with collaborative papers strongly encouraged.

Guidelines for abstracts

Abstracts could be submitted through one of two streams. Each abstract was required to contain 250–500 words, excluding references, and follow the EJOSSAH author guidelines.

A. Academic papers

  1. Cover page with paper title, names, institutional affiliations, contact addresses and biographies of up to 150 words.
  2. Paper title.
  3. Study type: empirical, interpretive, ethnographic, historical, philosophical or hermeneutic.
  4. Purpose or objective.
  5. Method, including population and sample where applicable.
  6. Key findings or preliminary results.
  7. Conclusion and implications.
  8. Five to six keywords.

B. Reflections on Practice

  1. Cover page with title, names, affiliations, contact details and short biographies.
  2. Title of the reflection.
  3. Area of practice: population, engagement and period.
  4. Level of ABCD implementation.
  5. Author reflection on documented community-level changes.
  6. Challenges and opportunities.
  7. Concluding remarks grounded in field experience.

Development of full papers

Selected contributions in both streams were expected to evolve over time. Authors without a complete manuscript were encouraged to submit an abstract and work with the guest editors following selection.

Q&A information session

A Zoom session was scheduled for 16 June 2025 to clarify submission guidelines, discuss the thematic focus and answer questions about abstract review and next steps. Contributors were asked to register by email no later than 10 June 2025.

  • Clarification of academic-paper and reflection-on-practice requirements.
  • Discussion of the special issue’s thematic focus.
  • Questions on abstract review and the development of full papers.
