Marketing as Culture: An Applied Anthropological Study of Esteh Indonesia’s Franchise Business Strategy

● Safriadi Safriadi, Arini Widyastuti, Wahyu Chandra

https://doi.org/10.4314/erjssh.v13i1.1

Abstract: This study examines how Esteh Indonesia prepares, communicates and implements its franchise marketing strategy. Based on qualitative interviews and documentary evidence, it shows that product quality, promotion, organizational management and socio-cultural factors shape a consistent but adaptive strategy that supports sales growth and consumer loyalty.

Determinants of Farmers’ Crop Management Strategies to Climate Change in Northwest Ethiopia: The Role of Agro-ecological Zones

● Menberu Teshome Zeleke

https://doi.org/10.4314/erjssh.v13i1.2

Abstract: Using survey data from 525 farm households and seven binary logistic regression models, this study identifies the distinct factors influencing crop-management adaptation choices in Northwest Ethiopia. Agro-ecology and farmer-to-farmer extension emerge as strong influences, while drought, land degradation, water scarcity, limited finance and weak formal extension constrain adaptation.

Article Details

Keywords: Climate change; Adaptation; Crop management strategies; Farmers’ choice; Agro-ecological zone; Northwest Ethiopia; Binary logistic regression

Ethiopian Female Labor Migration to the Middle East Countries: An Investigation of Migrants’ Readiness Level Prior to Departure

● Mengistu Dagnew Moges

https://doi.org/10.4314/erjssh.v13i1.3

Abstract: A mixed-method study of 224 returning women migrant domestic workers finds generally low pre-departure awareness and no significant preparedness advantage among legal migrants. The results call for effective orientation and greater support for migrants to make informed decisions before departure.

Author Guideline

ERJSSH accepts original research, review articles and related scholarly contributions in English. Submit one anonymized manuscript for review and one complete file with author names and affiliations. Work must be original, unpublished and not under review elsewhere.

Manuscripts should normally not exceed 8,000 words or 25 pages, use 12-point sans-serif type, 1.5 spacing, 2.5 cm margins and APA 7th edition references. Provide a 140–200 word abstract, five to eight keywords, consecutively numbered tables and figures, and all required ethics and permissions documentation.

There is no article processing charge. International authors may be handled under a separate support policy; contact the ERJSSH Editorial Office for guidance.

Reviewers Guideline

Reviewers assess originality, method, evidence, argument, ethical integrity and contribution. Reports must be objective, constructive and confidential. Conflicts of interest and suspected plagiarism, fabrication, duplicate publication or other misconduct must be reported privately to the editor.

Recommendations are acceptance, minor revision, major revision or rejection. Every research article is evaluated through double-blind review by at least two independent specialists.

Editorial Process
  1. Administrative, originality and scope screening.
  2. Preliminary assessment by the assigned editor.
  3. Double-blind review by two independent specialists.
  4. Author revision and response to reviewer reports.
  5. Final editorial decision, copy-editing and production.
  6. Author approval of the final version before publication.

The number of articles may increase or decrease between issues; acceptance is determined by scholarly quality and successful review, not by a fixed quota.

About the Journal

Aims, access and review

The Ethiopian Renaissance Journal of Social Sciences and Humanities (ERJSSH) publishes rigorous multidisciplinary scholarship in anthropology, sociology, geography, history, international relations, literature, linguistics, media and communication, political science, psychology, social work, gender studies and related fields.

ERJSSH is 100% open access and charges no APC. Two selected issues are published each year. Article numbers may vary, while all research papers remain subject to preliminary editorial screening and double-blind review by at least two independent reviewers.

English is the journal’s only publication language. International authors may be supported under a separate policy; contact the ERJSSH Editorial Office for current guidance.

Data governance

Privacy Statement

ERJSSH protects the personal information required for peer-reviewed publishing, including author and reviewer identity, affiliation, account, manuscript, review and editorial correspondence data.

Collection and use

Information is used only to manage submission, review, editorial decisions, publication, requested alerts, service improvement and the integrity of the scholarly record. Processing is based on consent, publishing obligations, legitimate scholarly interests and applicable law.

Sharing and security

Information may be shared only with reviewers, editors and service providers necessary for journal operations and subject to confidentiality and data-protection duties. ERJSSH does not sell or rent personal data. Reasonable organizational and technical safeguards are applied against unauthorized access, use or disclosure.

Your rights

Data subjects may request access, correction or deletion, object to certain processing, and withdraw consent where consent is the applicable legal basis. Material changes will be communicated through an updated statement on this page.
