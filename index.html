About the Journal

Rigorous enquiry, grounded evidence and original interpretation.

EJOSSAH was officially launched in 2003 as the journal of the College of Social Sciences, Addis Ababa University.

The journal publishes original work based on primary empirical data—including surveys, interviews, focus group discussions, archives, newspapers and institutional documents—as well as secondary-data studies that make a clearly defined new contribution or reinterpretation.

Its publication formats include research articles, book reviews, short communications, obituaries and conference papers. All research articles undergo editorial screening and double-blind peer review.
