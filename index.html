For contributors

Guidelines

ERJSSH accepts original research articles, review articles, book reviews, case reports, systematic reviews, scoping reviews and other scholarly contributions. Submission and publication are free of charge.

Authors’ guideline

Manuscript preparation

Submit two manuscript versions: one anonymized for review and one containing author names, affiliations and required contact details. A submission must be original, unpublished and not under consideration elsewhere.

Manuscripts should be in English, supplied in DOC, DOCX or RTF format, and normally not exceed 8,000 words or 25 typed pages including tables, notes and references. Use 12-point type, 1.5 line spacing, 2.5 cm margins and APA 7th edition referencing.

Abstract and keywords

Provide a concise abstract of 140–200 words and five to eight keywords. The abstract should communicate the background, objective, method, principal results and conclusion.

Tables, figures and photographs

Number tables and figures consecutively, cite each in the manuscript and provide clear captions and sources. Image files should be supplied at 300 dpi in TIFF, JPG or PNG format. Authors are responsible for reproduction permissions.

Authorship and research integrity

Authorship is limited to substantial scholarly contributions. All co-authors must approve the final manuscript and its submission. Research involving human participants requires appropriate ethical review and documented informed consent.

Reviewers’ guideline

ERJSSH uses double-blind peer review. Reviewers must assess work objectively, preserve confidentiality, declare conflicts of interest, identify uncited relevant scholarship and provide evidence-based recommendations. Personal criticism is not acceptable.

Recommendations are recorded as acceptance, minor modification, major modification or rejection. Suspected plagiarism, fabrication, duplicate publication or other misconduct must be reported confidentially to the editorial office.

Book review guideline

Book reviews should identify the work’s scholarly context, summarize its central argument, evaluate its evidence and method, and explain its contribution to the social sciences or humanities. Reviews should be analytical, fair and written for an interdisciplinary readership.

Editorial process
  1. Administrative and scope screening.
  2. Preliminary editorial assessment.
  3. Double-blind review by at least two independent specialists.
  4. Author revision and response to reviewers.
  5. Editorial decision, copy-editing and production.
  6. Author approval of the final version before publication.

Submit to ERJSSH

Submissions

Manuscripts are submitted through the journal’s registered author workflow. Before beginning, prepare an anonymized manuscript, a complete author version, an abstract, keywords and all required ethics or permission documentation.

About the journal

Aims and scope

The Ethiopian Renaissance Journal of Social Sciences and Humanities (ERJSSH) disseminates high-quality, peer-reviewed research across the broad spectrum of the social sciences and humanities. It provides a multidisciplinary platform for scholarship grounded in real-world questions and committed to theoretical advancement and practical relevance.

The journal welcomes original research, reviews and conceptual contributions in anthropology, sociology, geography, history, international relations, literature, linguistics, music and film studies, media and communication, political science, psychology, social work and gender studies.

Peer review

All submissions pass preliminary editorial screening before review by at least two independent specialists. ERJSSH follows a double-blind process and communicates reviewer recommendations and required revisions to authors before a final editorial decision.

Open access

Published articles are freely accessible online. The journal supports non-commercial scholarly reading and distribution with appropriate attribution while preserving the integrity of the published record.

Data governance

Privacy Statement

ERJSSH is committed to protecting the privacy of its authors, reviewers, editors and readers. This statement explains how the journal collects, uses and safeguards personal information and outlines the rights associated with that data.

Information we collect

Information necessary for peer-reviewed publishing may include names, institutional affiliations, contact details, job titles, ORCID iDs, research interests, publication history, manuscript data, co-author details, peer-review reports and editorial correspondence.

How we use information

  • To manage submission, peer review, editorial decisions and publication.
  • To communicate with authors, reviewers and editors.
  • To deliver requested alerts or journal information.
  • To improve the journal service and understand the reach of published research.
  • To maintain an accurate public scholarly record.

Legal basis and sharing

Data is processed on the basis of consent, contractual obligations, legitimate scholarly-publishing interests and applicable legal requirements. Information may be shared only with reviewers, editors and service providers required for journal operations. ERJSSH does not sell or rent personal information for marketing.

Your rights

Data subjects may request access, correction or deletion of personal information, object to certain processing and withdraw consent where consent is the applicable basis.

Data security and policy changes

Reasonable organizational and technical safeguards are used to prevent unauthorized access, use or disclosure. Significant policy changes will be communicated by publishing an updated statement on this journal page.
