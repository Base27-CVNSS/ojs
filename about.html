About EJOSSAH

Purpose, scope, publishing model, editorial process and author information.

About the Journal

The Ethiopian Journal of the Social Sciences and Humanities is dedicated to promoting fresh scholarly enquiry on Ethiopia and the Horn of Africa.

EJOSSAH is devoted to empirical and analytical research and its application across the social sciences and humanities. It was officially launched in 2003 as the journal of the College of Social Sciences, Addis Ababa University.

The journal publishes original papers that have not been published previously. These include research using primary empirical data—surveys, in-depth interviews, key informant interviews, focus group discussions, archives, newspapers and governmental or non-governmental documents—as well as work based on secondary data. Secondary-data papers must clearly identify a new contribution or reinterpretation of the existing literature.

EJOSSAH also publishes book reviews, short communications, obituaries and conference papers.

Editorial Policy

Originality

Submissions must be original, unpublished and not under simultaneous consideration elsewhere. Authors are responsible for clearly establishing the contribution of the manuscript.

Peer review

All research articles undergo editorial screening followed by double-blind peer review. Editorial decisions consider scope, research integrity, methodological quality, analytical contribution and clarity.

Open access

EJOSSAH is an Open Access journal. Content is free to read, and the journal does not charge an article processing fee.

Language and frequency

Contributions are submitted in English, with English summaries. The journal publishes twice yearly, in June and December.

Submissions

Manuscripts and their metadata are submitted through the EJOSSAH portal powered by Open Journal Systems. Authors should review the checklist and formatting requirements before creating a submission.

Open the official OJS submission portal

Author Guidelines

  1. Prepare an original manuscript in English and remove identifying information where blind review requires it.
  2. Provide an informative title, abstract, keywords, author affiliations and complete contact metadata.
  3. State the research objective, methods, evidence, findings, contribution and implications clearly.
  4. Apply the journal’s required citation, reference, table and figure conventions consistently.
  5. Upload the manuscript and any supplementary files through OJS, then confirm each item in the submission checklist.

For the authoritative formatting rules, consult the EJOSSAH record on AJOL and the official OJS portal.
